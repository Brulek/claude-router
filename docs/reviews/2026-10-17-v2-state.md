## A/B results (stand-in run, 2026-10-17)

An in-session A/B (`2026-10-17-in-session-ab.md`) ran all eight tasks across four arms once each. Quality was flat (every arm passed every programmatic check; spick's planner won the judged plan by one point). Cost: haiku-bare $0.03, sonnet-bare $0.54, haiku-spick $1.01 (72% Opus), sonnet-free $1.78. So on short tasks spick beats a strong main session with the full tool list by 43% but loses to a single Sonnet or Haiku worker; the verdict/formal-writing-to-Opus rule is the cost driver and bought no measured quality here.
